Repair Bay Guide

How to Use the Repair Bay

If your ship won't repair, the problem is usually caused by an obstruction, incorrect positioning, or an outdated repair template. Follow the checklist below before assuming the Repair Bay is broken.

Repair Bay Checklist

1. Keep the Repair Block Unobstructed

Nothing can cover the Repair Block—not even glass. Make sure there are no blocks placed directly above it.

2. Clear the Entire Repair Area

The Repair Bay needs enough space for the ship's entire blueprint. Ground, ceilings, or nearby structures can prevent repairs.

3. Account for the Ship's Bounding Box

Every ship has an invisible bounding box that extends roughly 5–10 blocks beyond the wings, nose, and tail. This extra space must also remain clear.

4. Position the Ship Correctly

Center the ship over the Repair Pad and ensure there are no docked ships attached during the repair.

5. Update the Repair Template

Every time you modify your ship, press Save Repair Template. Otherwise, the Repair Bay restores the last saved version—or the original Factory Blueprint if no updated template exists.

Alternative Repair Options

Repair Station in Space

Building a Repair Station in orbit avoids many common obstruction issues caused by terrain and nearby structures.

Station Services

You can also use Repair Services available at compatible stations instead of repairing at your own base.

Quick Tip: The most common causes of Repair Bay failure are invisible bounding-box obstructions, blocks covering the Repair Block, and forgetting to save an updated Repair Template after modifying your ship.
