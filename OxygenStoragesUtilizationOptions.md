<h3>Oxygen Storage Types &amp; Utilization</h3>
<p>
                Empyrion has several different oxygen storage options, but they are not all used the same way.
                Some oxygen containers can be consumed directly by your character, while others are intended
                only for refilling Base or Vessel oxygen tanks.
            </p>
</div>
<div class="rule">
<h3>Available Oxygen Types</h3>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Emergency O₂ (White 3-Cylinder Pack)</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Name:</strong> Emergency O₂</li>
<li><strong>Description:</strong> Emergency O₂</li>
<li><strong>Crafted In:</strong> Personal Suit Constructor</li>
<li><strong>Created From:</strong> Water Bottles</li>
<li><strong>O₂ Capacity:</strong> 35 units</li>
<li><strong>Can Be Used:</strong> Directly by your character or added to a Base/Vessel O₂ Tank</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Small O₂ Bottle (Green &amp; Silver)</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Name:</strong> Small O₂ Bottle</li>
<li><strong>Description:</strong> Emergency O₂ Bottle</li>
<li><strong>Crafted In:</strong> Water/O₂ Condenser</li>
<li><strong>Created From:</strong> Energy supplied to the condenser</li>
<li><strong>Fuel Sources:</strong> Biofuel, Small Fuel Cells, Large Fuel Cells, or Fusion Cells</li>
<li><strong>O₂ Capacity:</strong> 75 units</li>
<li><strong>Can Be Used:</strong> Directly by your character or added to a Base/Vessel O₂ Tank</li>
</ul>
<p>
                    The Water/O₂ Condenser is a terrain device crafted in the Personal Suit Constructor using
                    only <strong>2 Iron Ore</strong>. Since it continues producing while you're away, it becomes
                    an excellent early and mid-game source of both Water Bottles and emergency oxygen.
                </p>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>O₂ Bottle (Blue)</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Name:</strong> O₂ Bottle</li>
<li><strong>Description:</strong> O₂ Value: 250</li>
<li><strong>Crafted In:</strong> Constructor</li>
<li><strong>Recipe:</strong> 1 Water Jug + 1 Steel Plate → 2 O₂ Bottles</li>
<li><strong>O₂ Capacity:</strong> 250 units</li>
<li><strong>Can Be Used:</strong> Base or Vehicle O₂ Tanks only</li>
</ul>
<p>
                    Blue O₂ Bottles require at least <strong>250 empty capacity</strong> in the receiving O₂ tank
                    and <strong>cannot be consumed directly</strong> by your character.
                </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="rule">
<h3>Quick Comparison</h3>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Personal Use vs Tank Refill</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>White Emergency O₂:</strong> ✔ Personal Use | ✔ Tank Refill</li>
<li><strong>Green/Silver Small O₂ Bottle:</strong> ✔ Personal Use | ✔ Tank Refill</li>
<li><strong>Blue O₂ Bottle:</strong> ✖ Personal Use | ✔ Tank Refill Only</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
<div class="note">
<strong>Early Game Recommendation:</strong>
            The Green/Silver Small O₂ Bottle is generally the most practical long-term emergency oxygen source.
            It holds more than twice the oxygen of the White Emergency O₂ Pack (75 vs. 35 units) and can be
            produced automatically by a Water/O₂ Condenser while you're exploring.
        </div>
<div class="warning">
<strong>Important:</strong>
            Blue O₂ Bottles cannot be consumed directly by your character. They are intended exclusively for
            refilling Base and Vehicle oxygen tanks that have at least 250 units of available capacity.
        </div>
</div>
