## Oxygen Storage Types & Utilization

Empyrion has several different oxygen storage options, but they are not all used the same way. Some oxygen containers can be consumed directly by your character, while others are intended only for refilling Base or Vessel O₂ Tanks.

</div>

<div class="rule">

## Available Oxygen Types

<div class="subsection">

#### Emergency O₂ (White 3-Cylinder Pack)

- **Name:** Emergency O₂
- **Crafted In:** Personal Suit Constructor
- **Created From:** Water Bottles
- **O₂ Capacity:** 35 units
- **Can Be Used:** Directly by your character or added to a Base/Vessel O₂ Tank

</div>

<div class="subsection">

#### Small O₂ Bottle (Green & Silver)

- **Name:** Small O₂ Bottle
- **Description:** Emergency O₂ Bottle
- **Crafted In:** Water/O₂ Condenser
- **Created From:** Energy supplied to the condenser
- **Fuel Sources:** Biofuel, Small Fuel Cells, Large Fuel Cells, or Fusion Cells
- **O₂ Capacity:** 75 units
- **Can Be Used:** Directly by your character or added to a Base/Vessel O₂ Tank

The **Water/O₂ Condenser** is a terrain device crafted in the Personal Suit Constructor using only **2 Iron Ore**. Since it continues producing while you're away, it becomes an excellent early and mid-game source of both Water Bottles and emergency oxygen.

</div>

<div class="subsection">

#### O₂ Bottle (Blue)

- **Name:** O₂ Bottle
- **Crafted In:** Constructor
- **Recipe:** 1 Water Jug + 1 Steel Plate → 2 O₂ Bottles
- **O₂ Capacity:** 250 units
- **Can Be Used:** Base or Vehicle O₂ Tanks only

Blue O₂ Bottles require at least **250 empty capacity** in the receiving O₂ Tank and **cannot be consumed directly** by your character.

</div>

</div>

<div class="rule">

## Quick Comparison

<div class="subsection">

#### Personal Use vs Tank Refill

| Oxygen Type | Personal Use | Tank Refill |
|-------------|:------------:|:-----------:|
| White Emergency O₂ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Green/Silver Small O₂ Bottle | ✔ | ✔ |
| Blue O₂ Bottle | ✖ | ✔ |

</div>

</div>

<div class="note">

**Early Game Recommendation:** The **Green/Silver Small O₂ Bottle** is generally the most practical long-term emergency oxygen source.

- Holds more than twice the oxygen of the White Emergency O₂ Pack (**75 vs. 35 units**).
- Can be produced automatically by a Water/O₂ Condenser while you're exploring.

</div>

<div class="warning">

**Important:** Blue O₂ Bottles **cannot** be consumed directly by your character.

They are intended exclusively for refilling Base and Vehicle O₂ Tanks that have at least **250 units** of available capacity.

</div>
