<div class="rule">
<h3>How to Use the Repair Bay</h3>
<p>
            If your ship won't repair, the problem is usually caused by an obstruction,
            incorrect positioning, or an outdated repair template. Follow the checklist below
            before assuming the Repair Bay is broken.
        </p>
</div>
<div class="rule">
<h3>Repair Bay Checklist</h3>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>1. Keep the Repair Block Unobstructed</h4>
<p>
                Nothing can cover the Repair Block—not even glass. Make sure there are no blocks
                placed directly above it.
            </p>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>2. Clear the Entire Repair Area</h4>
<p>
                The Repair Bay needs enough space for the ship's entire blueprint. Ground,
                ceilings, or nearby structures can prevent repairs.
            </p>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>3. Account for the Ship's Bounding Box</h4>
<p>
                Every ship has an invisible bounding box that extends roughly
                <strong>5–10 blocks</strong> beyond the wings, nose, and tail.
                This extra space must also remain clear.
            </p>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>4. Position the Ship Correctly</h4>
<p>
                Center the ship over the Repair Pad and ensure there are
                <strong>no docked ships</strong> attached during the repair.
            </p>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>5. Update the Repair Template</h4>
<p>
                Every time you modify your ship, press <strong>Save Repair Template</strong>.
                Otherwise, the Repair Bay restores the last saved version—or the original
                Factory Blueprint if no updated template exists.
            </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="rule">
<h3>Alternative Repair Options</h3>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Repair Station in Space</h4>
<p>
                Building a Repair Station in orbit avoids many common obstruction issues caused
                by terrain and nearby structures.
            </p>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Station Services</h4>
<p>
                You can also use Repair Services available at compatible stations instead of
                repairing at your own base.
            </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="note">
<strong>Quick Tip:</strong>
        The most common causes of Repair Bay failure are invisible bounding-box obstructions,
        blocks covering the Repair Block, and forgetting to save an updated Repair Template
        after modifying your ship.
    </div>
