# Empyrion Galactic Survival Guide Library

Welcome to the **Empyrion Galactic Survival Guide Library**. This collection brings together practical gameplay guides, survival tips, building mechanics, and technical references to help both new and experienced players understand the game's systems.

Whether you're learning how to survive your first day, optimize your base's solar power, or troubleshoot advanced building mechanics, these guides provide quick and reliable answers.
