<div class="section">
<div class="rule">
<h3>Oxygen Production Methods</h3>
<p>
                There are several ways to produce oxygen in Empyrion, each suited for different stages of the game.
                Some methods generate Water Jugs first, while others produce emergency oxygen directly.
            </p>
</div>
<div class="rule">
<h3>Production Methods</h3>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Water Extractor</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Type:</strong> Ground terrain device</li>
<li><strong>Placement:</strong> In water</li>
<li><strong>Requires:</strong> Power source</li>
<li><strong>Produces:</strong> Water Jugs</li>
</ul>
<p>
                    Water quality affects production speed, with a maximum water quality of
                    <strong>80%</strong>.
                </p>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Mining Ice</h4>
<ul>
<li>Mine Ice Blocks from Ice Asteroids.</li>
<li>Ice can also be mined on Snow Planets (currently the Snow Starter Planet).</li>
<li>Process the Ice into <strong>Water Jugs</strong> using a Portable Constructor.</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Water/O₂ Condenser</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Type:</strong> Ground terrain device</li>
<li><strong>Produces:</strong> Alternating Water Bottles and Green/Silver Small O₂ Bottles</li>
</ul>
<p>
                    Production speed depends entirely on the planet's global oxygen concentration.
                    Placement location does <strong>not</strong> affect efficiency.
                </p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="rule">
<h3>Using Water Jugs</h3>
<p>
                Water Jugs are best processed in a terrain-placed Portable Constructor.
            </p>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Option 1: Blue O₂ Bottles</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Recipe:</strong> 1 Water Jug + 1 Steel Plate</li>
<li><strong>Output:</strong> 2 Blue O₂ Bottles</li>
<li><strong>Total Oxygen:</strong> 500 O₂ Units (250 each)</li>
<li><strong>Use:</strong> Base and Vessel O₂ Tanks only</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Option 2: Water Bottles</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Recipe:</strong> 1 Water Jug</li>
<li><strong>Output:</strong> 10 Water Bottles</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
<div class="rule">
<h3>Using Water Bottles</h3>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Emergency Oxygen</h4>
<p>
                    Place Water Bottles in your personal inventory and use the
                    <strong>Personal Suit Constructor</strong> to craft
                    <strong>Emergency O₂ (White 3-Cylinder Pack)</strong>.
                </p>
</div>
<div class="subsection">
<h4>Food &amp; Farming</h4>
<p>
                    Water Bottles are also important crafting ingredients for:
                </p>
<ul>
<li>Nutrient Solution</li>
<li>Sprouts</li>
<li>Various food recipes in the Food Processor</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
<div class="note">
<strong>Early Game Tip:</strong>
            The Water/O₂ Condenser is one of the strongest early-game investments.
            It continuously produces both Water Bottles and Small O₂ Bottles while you're exploring,
            and its production speed depends only on the planet's oxygen concentration—not where you place it.
        </div>
</div>
