Personal Suit Constructor Guide

Personal Suit Constructor

The Personal Suit Constructor is unique because it uses materials directly from your inventory (not your toolbar) and can craft a limited selection of essential items. It is always available and is not tied to any armor equipped through an Armor Locker.

It can produce four main categories of items that are especially valuable during the early stages of a new game.

Available Crafting Categories

A) Food Production

Craft basic foods into more advanced food items, such as converting Plant Protein into Energy Bars.

B) Medical Supplies

Produce essential survival medicine including:

  • Bandages
  • Antibiotic medication
  • Antitoxic medication

C) Emergency Oxygen

A unique function exclusive to the Personal Suit Constructor: convert Water Bottles into Emergency O₂ Packs (White).

D) Exclusive Survival Devices

These six devices can only be crafted here and should be prioritized early in every new game.

  • 1. Survival Tool (Free)
    Can be crafted at any time and includes three modes accessed with Right Mouse Button: Defense, Salvage, and Mining Drill.
  • 2. Detector (Free)
    A handheld detector that can be crafted immediately.
  • 3. Survival Tent (10 Fiber)
    Place on land to create a respawn point. In Single Player, it also allows sleeping through the night.
  • 4. Portable Heater/Cooler (1 Iron Ore)
    Place nearby to offset environmental temperature by approximately 30°F.
  • 5. Water/O₂ Condenser (2 Iron Ore)
    Generates alternating Water Bottles and Silver/Green Emergency O₂ Bottles while powered.
  • 6. Portable Constructor (3 Iron Ore)
    One of the most valuable early-game devices. Requires no power, crafts many essential items, and can process:
    • Crushed Rock → Iron, Copper, and Silicon Ore (and Ingots)
    • Promethium Ore → Promethium Pellets or Small Fuel Cells
    • Wood → Fiber → Biofuel for early energy production
Early Game Tip: Prioritize crafting the Survival Tool, Detector, Survival Tent, Portable Heater/Cooler, Water/O₂ Condenser, and especially the Portable Constructor as soon as possible to significantly improve your chances of surviving the early game.
